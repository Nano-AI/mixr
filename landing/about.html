About For campuses & teams Text mixr

mixr is a campus organizer you text.

Tell it what you're into, in your own words. It looks for other UW students who want the same thing, or an event on campus that fits, and helps you meet up. It all happens in iMessage, so there's nothing to install.

How it works

  1. Text mixr what you're into: pickup soccer, a study group for 332, someone to see shows with.
  2. It finds people or an event that fits and asks if you're in.
  3. You say yes and it connects you. Numbers are swapped only when both sides say yes.

Privacy and safety

  • Your number is shared only when you and the other person both say yes. Until then, everything goes through mixr.
  • Text STOP anytime to stop all messages. Text HELP for help.
  • mixr is for UW students only, for now.
  • mixr uses AI (Claude, made by Anthropic) to read your texts and write its replies.
  • mixr is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Washington.

The privacy page covers what we collect, who processes it, and how to have it deleted.
