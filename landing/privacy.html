Privacy

Last updated October 17, 2026

What mixr collects, why, and who else handles it, in plain words.

What we collect

  • Your phone number, so mixr can text you.
  • Your UW email address, to send a one-time code that confirms you're a UW student.
  • The texts you send mixr.
  • The profile you give us: what you're into and anything else you tell mixr about yourself.

Why

To match you with people and events, and to send you invites.

Who processes it

  • Anthropic (Claude) reads your texts and writes mixr's replies.
  • OpenAI turns what you tell mixr into embeddings, a numeric form used to find good matches.
  • Photon delivers messages over iMessage.
  • An email provider sends your verification code.

When your number is shared

Only when you and another person both say yes. Until then, people you're matched with don't see your number, and everything goes through mixr.

Stopping and deleting

Text STOP to mixr anytime and it stops texting you. Text HELP for help.

To have your data deleted, email hello@joinmixr.club with the phone number you used. We delete your data when you ask.

Who it's for

mixr is for UW students who are 18 or older, for now. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Washington.

Questions go to hello@joinmixr.club.
