## 2. Sponsor and title tracks

### Photon (sponsored side track)
Sources: https://dubhacks.notion.site/26-Sponsored-Side-Tracks-5e3ff32c1d7e834d9844812256c9fa06 and the sponsor's page https://somethingreat.notion.site/Hackathon-Guidelines-for-Photon-2-0-3f8e443f36bb81f9893fed0d08a4c01d

- **Prompt:** "Build a Messaging Agent using Photon 2.0." Agents that join human-to-human conversations, social-context companions, multi-agent systems with humans, voice agents, tools that span iMessage / SMS / WhatsApp / Telegram / email / calls. Most creative and technically impressive agent wins.
- **Requirement:** "To qualify for the prize, your project must be built on Photon 2.0 and live on at least one Photon channel."
- **Judging:** Vision (push the frontier of hybrid intelligence), Craft (cohesive, responsive, tasteful), Depth (real-world context and edge cases), Traction (could run with real users tomorrow). "What great looks like" also lists human-in-the-loop collaboration, context that persists across time/channels/teammates, and safety/fallback behavior.
- **Prizes (per team):** iMessage Track Winner $700 = $400 cash + $300 Photon credits + fast-track to final interview round. Runner-up $300 = $200 cash + $100 credits. Best Voice Agent $400 = $300 cash + $100 credits (separate, standalone; voice on an SMS number).
- **Usable:**
  - Photon 2.0 beta at https://photon.co/beta, "free to use", no promo code. Dashboard: https://app.photon.codes/dashboard (create project, add channels).
  - Docs: https://photon.codes/docs/beta/api-reference ("still being written"), cookbooks https://photon.codes/docs/beta/cookbooks (voice via Retell, ElevenLabs, LiveKit, Twilio), SDK https://photon.codes/docs/spectrum-ts/introduction. Brand kit https://photon.codes/brand.
  - Voice: phone calls run on an SMS number, not iMessage. "Calls and group chat on iMessage both need a dedicated iMessage line, so for the hackathon, build voice on SMS." (relevant to mixr group threads)
  - Photon Discord (per sponsor page): https://discord.gg/KhCRKNsx55 and https://discord.gg/hBDgu5vytc (the page invites pre-build feedback and says it "will boost your chances of winning"). Photon is also at the career fair.
  - Example ideas on the sponsor page include "Team coordinator" and "Club producer: combine event scouting, group decisions, and a generated RSVP page", close to mixr.
- **Already known (Discord):** $100 credits per hacker, signup automatic with no code. Getting an SMS number asks for a card. A dedicated number for group threads needs the Business plan; no override confirmed. Submit by marking the Photon track on Devpost. API is messaging only, no LLM. Booth is in front, next to SpaceX.
- **Judging:** in person (Photon is on the in-person list).

## 4. General rules and deadlines
Sources: Hacker Guide hub (rules) and https://dubhacks.notion.site/26-Submissions-and-Judging-Overview-14eff32c1d7e837793e2018a30a234cb
- Hacking starts Sat Oct 17, 12:30 PM; no coding or building before then (ideating is fine). Work shown at judging only from 12:30 PM Sat to 10 AM Sun.
- Draft/initial Devpost submission mandatory by Sat 10:30 PM, declaring your tracks. Final by Sun Oct 18, 10:00 AM (hard lock; Devpost confirms 10:00am PDT). Video required; teams without a video "will not be allowed to judge".
- Team max 4; one project per hacker. Only submit to DubHacks '26 (other hackathons = disqualified).
- Code uploaded before the deadline and linked on Devpost (public GitHub recommended). 70-30 rule: at most 30% code not written during the hackathon.
- Tracks: exactly 1 title track (3 DubHacks + Visa), at most 3 sponsored side tracks, unlimited non-sponsored side tracks. Select tracks on Devpost.
- Judging: Sun 10:30 AM workshop, then 3 rounds of 50 minutes (about 3-4 hours) in HUB Ballroom; whole team on-site, stay at your table; pitch under 3 minutes; check your hacker profile (http://profile.dubhacks.co/) for your slot. At least 1 person must be present at judging to qualify. Visa main track judged virtually via video.
- Closing ceremony Sun 2:45 PM; DubCoins redemption Sat 8:00-8:45 PM and Sun 3:45-4:30 PM (HUB Ballroom). Meals and an overnight room in Gates Center G01 (no sleeping) are provided.
- Discord: https://discord.gg/HBDh5u4gX6 (workshops, mentorship, announcements, #ask-dh); info@dubhacks.co.
